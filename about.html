Who We Are

Birmingham's Trusted
Catering Equipment Supplier

Catering Solutionz is Birmingham's trusted supplier of premium catering equipment and professional kitchen supplies, proudly serving the region for over 15 years.

Based in Aston, Birmingham, we supply restaurants, event caterers, hotels, food businesses, and private clients with everything they need to run a successful catering operation. Our extensive range covers stainless steel chafing dishes, commercial cookware, cutlery, crockery, glassware, disposables and much more — all chosen for quality, durability and outstanding value.

We believe that professional catering starts with the right equipment. That's why every product we stock is commercial-grade, built to withstand the demands of a busy kitchen or event. We combine fast local delivery, flexible payment options, and genuine expert advice to make sourcing your catering supplies as easy as possible.

Our Mission & Values

Everything we do is built on four core principles that set us apart.

Quality First

We only stock commercial-grade equipment that meets the highest standards of durability and performance. If it wouldn't stand up in a professional kitchen, it doesn't make it onto our shelves.

Honest Service

We give straightforward advice tailored to your actual needs — no unnecessary upselling, no jargon. Just honest guidance from people who understand the catering industry.

Fast & Reliable

We know downtime costs money. That's why we keep strong stock levels and offer prompt courier and palletised delivery across Birmingham and surrounding areas.

Great Value

Premium quality shouldn't mean premium prices. We work hard to offer the best value on the market, with flexible payment options including card and cash on delivery.
