Catering Solutionz is Birmingham's trusted supplier of premium catering equipment and professional kitchen supplies.

Based in Aston, Birmingham, we have been serving restaurants, event caterers, hotels and food businesses for over 15 years. Our extensive range — from stainless steel chafing dishes and commercial cookware to disposables and glassware — is chosen for quality, durability and value.

We offer fast local delivery across Birmingham, flexible payment options, and a team that genuinely understands the catering industry.
